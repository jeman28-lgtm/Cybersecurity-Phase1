1. What Nmap flag did you use to find the specific software versions running on these ports?
Answer: The Nmap flag we used is -sV 

2. Why is it dangerous to run an intense scan (like -A) on a fragile legacy system?
Answer: The system may become so overwhelmed trying to respond to the scan that it stops responding to legitimate users or crashes entirely. This is effectively a self-inflicted DoS (Denial of Service) attack.
